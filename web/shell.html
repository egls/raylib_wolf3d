Controls
Arrows move, Ctrl fires, Space opens doors, Alt strafes, Shift runs, 1–4 pick a weapon, Esc is the menu (save and load are there). Click the game to use the mouse (left fires, right strafes, middle opens); Esc lets go and pauses. Alt+Enter: fullscreen. Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half to move and turn (with STRAFE held, sideways). Right thumb: FIRE (hold for auto-fire), USE opens doors, RUN on/off, ‹ › change weapon; drag on the right half to turn. MENU is Esc (save and load are there). In menus tap the picture for Enter, swipe or use the arrows to move, Y/N answer questions, ⌨ types a save name.
